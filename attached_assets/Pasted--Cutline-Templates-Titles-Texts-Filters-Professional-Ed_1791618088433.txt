## 4. Professional Editing Tools — Audit and Complete Missing Features

I cannot tell from the current UI alone whether Cutline already has proper cutting and trimming tools. Inspect the implementation and compare it with the essential workflow of professional desktop video editors.

### Essential timeline editing

* Split/cut a clip at the playhead.
* Trim the beginning or end of a clip.
* Move, select, and delete clips.
* Move and arrange clips on the timeline.
* Support multiple video, audio, and text tracks where the current architecture allows it.
* Allow users to adjust clip duration and position accurately.
* Provide undo and redo for editing actions.
* Support zooming and horizontal scrolling through the timeline.
* Show a clear playhead and accurate time ruler.
* Prevent accidental deletion or movement of clips during normal interaction.

### Preview and playback

* Play, pause, and seek through the project.
* Keep the preview synchronized with the timeline and playhead.
* Ensure titles, text, filters, and effects appear correctly during playback.
* Fix any existing preview issues without breaking project settings or export quality.

### Audio editing

* Adjust clip volume.
* Mute or unmute clips.
* Trim and split audio clips.
* Display an audio waveform if supported by the current architecture.
* Keep audio and video synchronized.

### Professional workflow

* Add keyboard shortcuts for common actions, including Split, Undo, Redo, Delete, Play/Pause, and Save.
* Add snapping and useful timeline selection controls where appropriate.
* Make sure project saving and reopening preserve clip positions, durations, effects, text, and track arrangement.
* Keep export settings and output quality consistent with the existing implementation.
